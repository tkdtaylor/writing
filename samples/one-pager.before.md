# Self-Serve Merchant Webhook Replay Proposal

In today's interconnected commerce landscape, event-driven extensibility is pivotal for enterprise velocity. Our event delivery pipeline serves as a cornerstone of merchant integration workflows and stands as a testament to platform extensibility, highlighting our commitment to operational excellence. The implementation of self-serve controls accelerates the optimization of partner onboarding during the transformation of merchant operations. The developer console already boasts over 50 configuration controls — yet workspace admins still cannot replay dropped events themselves, ensuring frustration across partner ecosystems.

Here's the kicker: between September 1 and September 30, transient merchant `5xx` timeouts dropped `14,200` webhook delivery batches across active storefronts. The result? Support queues back up whenever transient partner outages drop webhook batches. That volume generated `68` manual replay tickets (`38%` of weekly Integration Support load) — forcing on-call engineers to spend `11 hours/week` running ad-hoc `replay-dlq.sh` scripts with a `4.6-hour` median turnaround before `180` Q4 holiday workspaces launch ahead of our November 1 code freeze. Why does this matter? Delayed order events stall merchant fulfillment teams. This isn't a tooling issue. It's a developer experience paradigm shift. Full stop.

Our proposed replay architecture accelerates merchant recovery without sacrificing reliability. The dead-letter gateway scales globally without compromising security. The replay experience behaves less like an admin utility and more like a self-healing flywheel. Let's unpack the core pillars of this holistic initiative:

- **Scalability:** Scalability across the webhook substrate is bolstered when teams unleash self-serve replay workflows via `POST /v1/webhooks/replays` and an encrypted `s3://events-dlq-prod/` bucket (`72-hour` TTL, `KMS` encryption, `8` engineer-days, `$190/month` in `S3` and `SQS` spend) capped at `50 RPS` per endpoint.
- **Resilience:** Resilience improves seamlessly from reactive firefighting to proactive empowerment — while leaving payload schema mapping, archival past `72` hours, and our `5`-attempt `30`-minute live retry schedule out of scope.
- 🚀 Holistic replay orchestration streamlines partner onboarding across the realm compared to storing `64 KB` JSON bodies in a synchronous `events_prod` Postgres table (`5` engineer-days, `$640/month` `RDS` cost, heavy `WAL` lag) or keeping manual `replay-dlq.sh` status quo tickets (`$0` infra spend, `11` engineer-hours/week).

At the end of the day, dedicated `webhook-replay-workers` and our `webhooks.self_serve_replay` feature flag will foster seamless operations — halting new replays in `15` seconds if worker `CPU` passes `75%` or live `p95` delivery latency exceeds `2.0` seconds. Simple as that.
